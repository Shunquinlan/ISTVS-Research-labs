Lab services

Testing for industry and research partners

Single-purpose projects follow ASTM and ASABE procedures. Longer projects can be internal, government-sponsored or commercially sponsored.

Soil characterization

  • Moisture content and Atterberg limits
  • Triaxial and direct shear
  • Proctor compaction
  • Shear vane, cone penetrometer, bevameter

Machine performance

  • Tire and track traction and rolling resistance
  • Soil compaction and rut depth
  • Scaled tool draft and soil flow

Wear and materials

  • Accelerated tool wear in abrasive media
  • Hardened and coated tool comparison
  • Test soils: artificial soil, Clarion loam, sand, clay, pea gravel, limestone gravel, or your own
