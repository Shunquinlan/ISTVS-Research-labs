## 1. The existing lab data (Knack) — highest priority

1. Can the Secretariat provide an **export of the current Research Facilities
   database** from Knack (Excel, CSV, or JSON)? This is needed to design the
   spreadsheet in Week 2.
2. **How many research labs** are currently in the database?
3. What **fields / columns** does the current record have? (We are assuming: lab
   name, institution, location, research category, principal investigator,
   description, capabilities / research areas, website, contact.)
4. The Knack database stores **assets** — lab images and PDF documents. Should the
   new site display lab photos and/or link attached documents? If so, roughly how
   many labs have images today?
5. Is there an agreed **list of research categories**, or do we define one?
6. Any data that should **not** be published publicly (e.g. personal emails,
   internal notes)?
