Modeling and simulation

Tests that calibrate models

Every test produces data for physics-based models of soil–machine interaction. The lab calibrates discrete element method (DEM) models for soil, gravel and grain, and uses them for virtual design of tools and traction devices.

  • DEM models of bulldozer-blade and cultivator-sweep interaction with soil
  • Worn tools are 3D scanned and brought back into simulation to predict draft and vertical forces
  • Controlled test data supports training of algorithms for automated off-road machines
  • Tire footprints and rut measurements link contact area to traction and compaction
