Courses

ABE 340

Functional Analysis of Soil, Crop, and Machine Systems

Principles of machine systems operation (tillage, crop establishment, harvesting and crop protection). Principles of soil and crop interactions with machine systems. Experimental and simulation techniques for testing and evaluation of agricultural field machinery for equipment performance, functional analysis and crop production management.

TSM 330

Agricultural Machinery and Power Management

Selection, sizing, and operational principles of tractors and machinery systems. Cost analysis and computer techniques applied to planning and management of agricultural machine systems. Principles, operation, and application of agricultural machinery.

A B E 440X / 540X

Modeling and Simulation of Soil-Crop-Machine Systems

Principles of soil and crop material characterization, constitutive relationships for modeling soil and crop material behaviors, and applied computational and experimental methods for soil-crop-machine interactions for design and evaluation of off-road machine systems.

  • 2024
    Integrating DEM and Scaling Law Modeling Techniques for Simulation of Earth-Moving Applications (Mehari Tekeste, Mohamed Abdeldayem), Machine-Ground Interaction Consortium, University of Wisconsin–Madison
    Five geometrically scaled bulldozer blades (λ = 1/8 to 1/15 of a Caterpillar D3k2XL blade) were tested in the soil bin on cohesive-frictional artificial soil. Scaling law models related soil horizontal forces (R² = 0.9898) and soil mass (R² = 0.9951) to blade scale, and the calibrated DEM soil model predicted soil reaction forces with RMSE of 14.89 N (straight blade) and 7.42 N (curved blade).
    Journal article · MaGIC 2024

Agronomy & engineering outreach: tillage, soil compaction and traction

  • 2024
    Iowa State Association of Counties (ISAC) Annual Conference, Educational Seminar: Iowa State University Soil Compaction Study
    Drs. Mehari Tekeste and Mark Hanna discuss research on soil impacts and cropland recovery from heavy equipment, such as during pipeline construction, including the potential for excessive soil compaction under different soil moisture conditions.
    ISAC article (PDF)
  • 2024
    Soil Compaction Management: Field Machinery Traffic and Impacts on Soil Physical Properties and Crop Yield, 2024 Crop Management Conference, University of Missouri, Columbia, MO
    The effect of machine-induced soil compaction on soil stress under dynamic tire and track vehicle passes, soil physical properties (soil bulk density and soil cone index) and crop yields, investigated in central Iowa.
