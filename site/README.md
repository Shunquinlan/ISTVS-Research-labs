# ISTVS Research Labs

A public directory of research laboratories working in terrain-vehicle systems
and terramechanics, maintained by the International Society for Terrain-Vehicle
Systems (ISTVS) together with the Iowa State University ISTVS student chapter.

{% hint style="info" %}
This site is still being built. The lab directory and the individual lab profile
pages are planned for weeks 3–5 of the project.
{% endhint %}

## What will be here

* A browsable directory of research labs — by name, institution, location, and
  research category
* A profile page for each lab, with its capabilities, principal investigator,
  and contact details
* Simple category navigation

## Who it is for

Academia, industry, students, federal laboratories, and ISTVS members.
