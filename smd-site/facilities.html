Facilities

Three soil bins, one controlled environment

Field conditions change from hour to hour, and Iowa fields close for winter. In the lab, soil is prepared to a set condition before every run, so two tires, tools or materials are compared on equal ground. The lab is at the ISU Agricultural Engineering and Agronomy Research Farm near Boone, Iowa.

01 · Mobility

Single-tire mobility soil bin

Tires and tracks for agricultural and construction machines are tested in towed or powered runs with slip control. A processing carriage tills, levels and rolls the artificial soil to a uniform bed or to compacted layers before each run.

Bin size27 m × 1.4 m × 0.5 m deep
SoilArtificial cohesive-frictional soil (sand, clay, oil)
Bulk density1.2–1.7 Mg/m³
Load cells11 kN long. · 20 kN vert. · 11 kN lat.
Wheel sensingSpinning wheel force transducer, 3-axis forces and moments
Test tire sizeUp to 0.7 m wide, 1.2 m overall diameter
Test modesTowed or powered · vertical load control · steering angle
Soil checksCone penetrometer · shear · Bekker sinkage · rut depth
02 · Wear

Circular soil bin for tool wear

Cultivator sweeps, harrow tines, ripper points and small discs are worn in an abrasive medium at speeds the field cannot reach. After each pass the medium is re-leveled, so both test stations see the same conditions. The bin has reproduced a typical farm replacement cycle of about 2,000 acres for cultivator sweeps.

Drum4.8 m outer diameter · 0.4 m deep
Drive37 kW (50 hp) electric motor
Arm speed0–5.4 m/s, both directions
Stations2 tool stations (180° apart) + 2 processing arms
MediumPea gravel with timed water spray
ControlSpeed and depth change on the go
Wear measuresMass loss · dimensions · 2D image area · 3D scan
03 · Ground-engaging tools

Linear soil bin for scaled tools

Scaled bulldozer blades, loader buckets, ripper teeth and grader blades are driven through prepared soil while 3-axis forces, moments and soil rupture are recorded. Results are scaled to full-size machines using similitude laws.

Bin size15 m × 1.5 m × 0.25 m deep
MotionServo control in travel and depth, plus rotation
ControllerSimulink / LabVIEW on NI PXI real-time
Tool scalee.g. 1/8-scale bulldozer blade
Measures3-axis forces · moments · soil flow
